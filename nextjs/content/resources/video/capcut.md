## What it's for

CapCut is for short, vertical video with captions on screen, made fast: TikToks, Reels, Shorts, a trailer for your project. It has a multi-track timeline, keyframes, speed ramps, chroma key, thousands of templates, effects, stickers, and music, and automatic captions that look like the ones on social media. Pick it over iMovie when the piece is vertical, needs styled captions, or will go to social media, or when you want to edit on your phone and finish on a laptop. Pick iMovie (or a pro editor) when the material is an interview you've promised to keep private, when you need to hand a timeline to another editor, or when you don't want to make an account.

## The tour

CapCut's desktop layout (verify labels against your version; CapCut moves things between updates):

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home screen** | New project, your drafts, templates, and the cloud space. You need to sign in for most of it. |
| **Media panel** | Top left. Tabs along the top for Media (your imports), Audio, Text, Stickers, Effects, Transitions, Captions, Filters, Adjustment, Templates, and AI tools. |
| **Player** | Top center. Set the canvas ratio here (16:9, 9:16, 1:1). |
| **Details panel** | Top right. Settings for the selected clip: Video (transform, Remove BG, mask, chroma key), Audio, Speed, Animation, Adjustment. |
| **Timeline** | Along the bottom. The main track holds your story; drag clips, text, and stickers onto tracks above it. |

## The basic edit

1. **New project:** on the home screen, click Create project. Set the ratio in the player (9:16 for vertical) before you start.
2. **Import:** click Import in the media panel, or drag files in.
3. **Build the sequence:** drag clips to the main track. Click the + on a clip to add it at the end.
4. **Trim and split:** drag the ends of a clip. Put the playhead where you want a cut and press Command-B (verify) or click the split button above the timeline.
5. **Sound:** add music from Audio, or drag your own. Select a clip and use the Audio tab in the details panel for volume and noise reduction.
6. **Captions:** Captions > Auto captions, choose the spoken language, Generate. Then read every caption and fix names and mistakes.
7. **Text, effects, transitions:** drag them in. Templates and effects marked Pro will put a watermark on your export unless you pay; check before you build around one.
8. **Export:** click Export (top right). Choose resolution, frame rate, and format.

## AI in the app

Most of CapCut's AI runs on CapCut's servers, not on your laptop, and many of these features need a paid plan or use credits. Which ones are free changes from update to update.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto captions** | Transcribes speech into styled captions on the timeline. Accuracy drops with accents, fast speech, and names; fix them by hand. |
| **Remove BG** | Details panel > Video > Remove BG > Auto removal: cuts a person out of the background without a green screen. **Chroma key** is there too, for real green screen. |
| **AI remove** | Paint over an object and CapCut fills it in. |
| **Noise reduction and vocal isolation** | Cleans up speech or separates the voice from music (vocal isolation is a Pro feature, verify). |
| **Text to speech and AI voices** | Generates a voiceover from typed text. |
| **Long video to shorts** | Suggests short clips from a long recording. |
| **AI video and image generation** | Makes clips and images from prompts; uses credits. |

Be careful with AI voices and generative fills on interview or documentary material, as with any editor: changing what a real person said or what was in the shot is a decision to make on purpose and to disclose.

## What to know before you upload

CapCut's [Terms of Service](https://www.capcut.com/clause/terms-of-service) (last updated April 15, 2026) say that by submitting content you grant TikTok USDS Joint Venture and its "affiliates, agents, services providers, partners and other connected third parties an unconditional, non-exclusive, royalty-free, fully transferable, sub-licensable, perpetual and worldwide license to use your User Content," including to "modify, adapt, reproduce, make derivative works of, display, publish, transmit, distribute and/or store" it "for the purposes of operating, developing and providing the Services, subject to your Platform settings." They also grant a license to use your username, image, and likeness "including for use in sponsored content." The service is for people 13 and older.

What this means for this course:

- **Interviews and other people's faces and words:** don't upload footage that someone gave you on a promise of where it would appear, unless they agree to it going through CapCut. Use iMovie or one of the pro editors, which keep your media on your Mac.
- **Your own work:** you keep ownership, but the license is broad and doesn't expire. Decide whether that's fine for a given piece.
- **Data:** US data is meant to be held under the joint venture's arrangements (hosted in Oracle's US cloud, per [reporting on the joint venture](https://www.hooked.so/guides/is-capcut-getting-banned)). How fully it is separated from ByteDance hasn't been independently confirmed.
- **Availability in the US:** CapCut went offline in the US with TikTok on January 19, 2025, came back to the app stores in February 2025, and since the joint venture closed on January 22, 2026 it has been available normally. TikTok's [joint venture announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/announcement-from-the-new-tiktok-usds-joint-venture-llc?lang=en) says its safeguards "will also cover CapCut, and Lemon8." There's no separate "CapCut US" app. As of October 21, 2026, the app is in the US App Store.
- **Harvard:** CapCut isn't one of Harvard's licensed tools, and HUIT hasn't reviewed it for confidential data (verify with HUIT before using it for anything beyond your own material).

## Working with Claude

CapCut has no scripting interface and doesn't export a timeline file, so Claude works on the files around it.

**Before:** ask Claude to make a paper cut from a transcript (see [the video overview](README.md#paper-cut-from-a-transcript)) and give you a list of in/out times to cut by hand. Or have ffmpeg render the rough cut, then bring that one file into CapCut for captions and finishing.

**Captions you control:** instead of uploading to CapCut for auto captions, transcribe locally with Whisper (see the [audio AI tools](../audio/ai-tools.md)), ask Claude to fix the SRT, and import it into CapCut (Captions > Import, verify) or add it after export with [ffmpeg](../cli/ffmpeg.md).

**After:** check the export with `ffprobe` and recompress or crop it with [ffmpeg](../cli/ffmpeg.md). A 16:9 export cropped to a centered 9:16 (tested with ffmpeg 9.0.2):

```bash
ffmpeg -i movie.mp4 -vf "crop=ih*9/16:ih,scale=1080:1920" -c:a copy movie_vertical.mp4
```

Example prompt:

```text
Here is interview.srt from my 4-minute interview. Pick 45 seconds that would
work as a vertical teaser, list the in/out times in order, and write a
teaser.srt with just those lines, retimed to start at 0, under 32 characters
per line so CapCut-style captions stay on screen.
```

## Export settings that work

Click Export (top right), then:

| Setting | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Resolution | 1080p. 4K needs a paid plan (Pro, verify which tier). |
| Frame rate | The same as your footage (30 for most phone video) |
| Format | MP4 |
| Codec | H.264 for anywhere; HEVC is smaller but less widely supported |

**Watermarks:** a free account exports without a watermark as long as the project uses only free assets. Any Pro-tagged template, effect, sticker, sound, or AI clip adds a watermark to the whole export, according to [third-party testing](https://pixflow.net/blog/capcut-pro-price-worth-it/) (verify by exporting a short test). Templates exported from the phone app may add an ending clip with the CapCut logo; delete it from the end of the timeline.
